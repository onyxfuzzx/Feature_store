# Community conduct

Participants in issues, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces should be
respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Critique ideas and code with evidence;
allow people to correct mistakes and ask questions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, personal attacks,
and disclosure of someone else's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, close discussions,
or restrict participation. They should explain decisions where doing so respects
the privacy and safety of those involved. Apply these standards consistently,
including to maintainers.

Report conduct concerns privately through a maintainer's contact method listed
on their repository profile. Include links and context; avoid posting private
information publicly. A maintainer involved in a report should defer review to
another maintainer where one is available. This volunteer project does not
promise a response deadline.
